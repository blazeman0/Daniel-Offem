Brain-computer interface robotic car

A student team prototype exploring how EEG signals could provide hands-free commands to a small robotic vehicle.

As team leader, I worked on integrating a NeuroSky MindWave EEG headset with an Arduino-controlled car. The project combined signal acquisition, Python-based processing, machine-learning experiments, and embedded hardware to translate selected signals into movement.

MY ROLETeam leader
REPORTED RESULT~78% left/right accuracy
SHOWCASEGBY Kıbrıs Project Market

System components

NeuroSky MindWaveThinkGear Connector PythonMATLABScikit-learn SVMRandom ForestArduino L298N motor driverHC-06 Bluetooth

Prototype command logic

Attention above 60Move forward
Meditation above 60Move backward
Attention below 30Stop
Blink signal / double blinkTurn left / right

Challenges & learning

The team worked through inconsistent EEG signal quality, intermittent zero-valued readings, headset-to-computer communication, serial and Bluetooth links, and Python-to-Arduino integration. The project was presented at the Genç Beyinler Yeni Fikirler – Kıbrıs Project Market and Joint Graduation Exhibition at Lefke European University.
